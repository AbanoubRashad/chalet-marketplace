# Listing a unit on site (inspector guide)

Takes about 30–40 minutes per unit, all from your phone.

1. **Before you go.** Make sure the owner has registered (`/list-your-chalet`) and finished "التحقق
   والمستندات": ID, payout details and the signed agreement.
2. **On arrival.** Log in at `/login` → **المعاينة** → "+ وحدة جديدة". Pick the owner and the village, write
   an honest title ("شاليه ٢ غرف صف تاني · النورس"), and enter capacity, rooms, baths, parking, nightly price
   and deposit. Then tap **إنشاء المسودة**.
3. **Photos, in order.** Tap **صوّر أو اختار صور**. The phone camera opens.
   - Take the façade or view first, then living room, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, terrace or beach.
   - Use daylight. Don't edit the photos.
   - Each upload is stamped "صورة فريقنا" with today's date.
   - Reorder with ↑ / ↓ and delete with ×. You need at least 3 photos.
4. **Checklist.** Tick only what you actually checked:
   - photos match the unit
   - air conditioners work
   - water heater
   - kitchen equipped
   - fire extinguisher and safe exit
   - key handover arrangement
   - cleanliness

   Add notes for anything the owner must fix.
5. **Approve.** Tap **اعتمد المعاينة**. The unit only becomes `inspected` when every item passes and there are
   3 or more photos. Otherwise it stays a draft with your notes.
6. **Publishing.** An admin publishes inspected units from **الإدارة → الوحدات** (`inspected → live`) after
   pricing and calendar sync are set up with the owner.
7. **Re-inspection.** Repeat a visit at least once per season, or after any guest issue of type "مش زي الوصف".
